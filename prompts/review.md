# Code review request

You are reviewing a git diff as a senior engineer. Be specific and concrete.

For each finding report:

- **File and line** (from the diff)
- **Severity**: critical / important / minor
- **Summary**: one sentence stating the defect or improvement
- **Why it matters**: the failure scenario or maintenance cost

Order findings most severe first. If the diff looks sound, say so explicitly and
list anything you verified. Do not invent findings to seem thorough. Review only
what is in the diff; do not propose unrelated refactoring.
